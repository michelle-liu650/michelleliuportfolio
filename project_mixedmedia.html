    Watch Animation

    Mixed Media Animation

    Overview

    An experiment in making the digital physical. I wanted to see how physically cutting and drawing on printed frames would change the look and feel of an animation.

    Process and Reflection

    Working by hand removed the safety net of undoing. Once I had cut or drawn on a frame, the mark was permanent, which pushed me to let go of perfectionism and embrace the handmade quality. With a limited number of frames, I became absorbed in inventing a different hairstyle for each one, and the continuity of the doodles loosened as a result. The underlying footage kept the sense of motion intact, and the piece became a study in how much a stable core image can hold together.

    Tools: DaVinci Resolve
