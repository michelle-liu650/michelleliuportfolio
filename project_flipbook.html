    Flipbook

    Overview

    This project explores how a small interaction can grow into something larger and more meaningful. Through three playful characters, I visualised the meeting of different ideas and the unexpected possibilities that emerge from connection.

    Concept

    When the characters collide, sparks appear, and their interaction begins to transform the world around them into an imaginative environment the viewer is invited into.

    Why a flipbook

    I’m drawn to design that brings ideas, people and experiences together, and to motion graphics for its ability to tell stories that audiences can feel immersed in. The flipbook turns movement and connection into a physical object, letting the viewer activate the animation and experience it first-hand.
