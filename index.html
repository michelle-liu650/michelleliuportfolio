    PURPOSE.

    Design Philosophy

    Designing stuff that invites people into a visual playground of possibilities. Design to me is about imagination, experience-making and connection.

    Bio

    I'm a design and commerce student at the University of Auckland, interested in the ways visual storytelling, motion and technology can come together to create meaningful experiences.

    I've always been drawn to stories. Growing up, books and films gave me places to escape to, characters to see myself in and worlds where I could feel like I belonged. I think that's what draws me to storytelling: the way something imagined can make us feel understood, connected or a little less alone. I'd love to create things that leave people with even a fragment of that feeling.

    When it comes to making, I like to dream big, approaching ideas with an open mind and seeing how far I can take them. I enjoy figuring things out along the way, experimenting, playing with different materials and putting things together until an idea becomes something tangible. There's something incredibly satisfying about seeing all those little pieces come together into something I can be proud of.

    For me, design is about imagination, empathy and play. I'm interested in how movement, visuals and interaction can invite people into an experience, spark curiosity and create connections. At the moment, I'm exploring motion design and UX/UI, figuring out how my interests in storytelling and technology might come together in my practice.

    I'm still discovering where design will take me, and I'm excited by the possibilities. For now, I'm here to keep experimenting, make things that mean something and see where a little curiosity can lead.

    Have a look around, and if something resonates with you, I'd love to connect!
