    PURPOSE.

    Stories made me feel like I belonged. I make new ones for the same reason.

    Growing up, books and films were my escape hatches: worlds to wander, characters to see myself in. Now I get to build those worlds myself, with whatever tools the story needs.

    I’m a design and commerce student at the University of Auckland, and a bit of a multidisciplinary designer. I'm currently diving deeper into motion design and UX/UI. I like to dream big, experiment, and piece things together until an idea becomes something real.

    Have a look around, and if something resonates, say hi!

    Michelle's Design Philosophy

    I design things that invite people into a visual playground of possibilities, built on imagination, experience and connection.
